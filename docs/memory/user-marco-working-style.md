Marco is a Microsoft Certified Trainer in Costa Rica who runs a certification-prep business with a partner (Spanish and English). They are technical, but want plain, short reports in prose, not walls of detail.

How they work with me:
- They answer decisions in short lines ("yes", "go with your leanings") and expect me to carry on; ask only when something is really theirs to decide.
- They want to **audit before anything is deployed** to Azure ("hold off on deploying the labs"). Never deploy, create real student resources or run anything that bills without being told.
- **Commit and push only when asked**, one phase at a time; they usually say "commit and push" after reviewing a summary. Never force-push without explicit words.
- For UI, they approve a plan before a larger pass (rule in `CLAUDE.md`).
- They like me to verify before claiming: run the tests, look at the screen in both themes and at phone width, say honestly what was not verified (nothing has run on real Azure yet).
- They corrected me once on a wrong assumption (the catalog is instructor demos, students do Microsoft Learn labs); when a request seems to conflict with the plan, say so.

**Why:** the stakes are a live business subscription and real students.

**How to apply:** open reports with what is done and whether it is committed; list what is unverified; end with the one next decision.
