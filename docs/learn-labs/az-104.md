# AZ-104: Azure Administrator labs

Source: [MicrosoftLearning/AZ-104-MicrosoftAzureAdministrator](https://github.com/MicrosoftLearning/AZ-104-MicrosoftAzureAdministrator)
(updated 2026-09-24). 14 labs under `Instructions/Labs/` (about 8 hours 45 minutes in all), 11 instructor demos under
`Instructions/Demos/`, and starter templates under `Allfiles/Labs/`. Read on 2026-10-04: all 14 lab files, by keyword
extraction of the resources, sizes, regions and permissions (not every step).

## What each lab deploys

Every lab says: "your subscription type may affect the availability of features", the steps are written for **East US**, and
"for each lab you create a new resource group".

| Lab | Time | Group it tells the student to create | What it creates and needs |

## What this means for guardrails

1. **A single resource group is not enough.** Eleven labs each ask for their own group (and lab 10 asks for two, in two regions).
   Some end by deleting it. A student needs a *set* of groups, created when each lab starts, named so they cannot collide
   (`az104-rg4` for every student cannot exist in one subscription).
2. **Contributor is not enough.** Lab 02b assigns policy and creates a lock on the student's own group. Lab 07 assigns roles to the
   student's own account. A student who can assign roles can give themselves more, so this needs a **constrained**
   role-assignment right (Azure supports conditions on role assignments that limit which roles may be assigned), tried in a test
   subscription before it is trusted. My inference, not verified: the *Inherit a tag* policy changes resources, so assigning it
   needs a managed identity with a role.
3. **Labs 01 and 02a cannot run in the business tenant.** They create users, invite guests and create management groups. Options:
   instructor demos (the repository's `Demos/01` and `Demos/02`), or each student creates a free tenant of their own (not verified
   for this setup). Do not give students directory rights in the business tenant.
4. **Quota is the real limit.** D2s_v5 is 2 vCPUs; labs 05, 06, 08, 10 and 11 each deploy two or more, plus a scale set. A few
   students at once can reach a typical regional vCPU limit, and Premium V3 App Service often starts at zero quota on a new
   pay-as-you-go subscription (lab 09a). Check the real numbers before the first AZ-104 cohort; stagger the labs.
5. **Cost comes from time, not size.** Each lab is 15 to 50 minutes and ends with "delete the group". The costly pieces are the
   Application Gateway (lab 06), the VMs and Premium disks (labs 05, 06, 08, 10, 11) and Site Recovery (lab 10). A student who forgets
   to delete is the risk: expiry and a budget per group are the brake.
6. **Regions.** East US throughout, East US and West US for lab 10; lab 06 once mentions North Europe (probably stale text).
7. **Overlap with Gaia's catalog.** Catalog labs 16 to 21 cover monitoring, storage administration, containers, backup, roles and
   Bicep as **instructor demos** of the same topics, so the instructor can show what a student then does by hand.
