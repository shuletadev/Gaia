# 07 · Invoice scanner

Blueprint `cr-facturas-escaner` · code `cfact` · **Status: Spec** · Batch 1

**Exam mapping:** AI-900 (computer vision workloads: OCR, document/receipt analysis, image analysis) · AI-900 (responsible AI considerations).

## Scenario
An accountant in Escazú receives supplier invoices as phone photos and PDFs and retypes them into a spreadsheet every
month. She wants the computer to read the date, supplier and total.

## Students learn
- Describe what OCR and "prebuilt document models" do, and what they cannot do.
- Analyze an invoice and a receipt in the studio and read the extracted fields with confidence scores.
- Explain why a confidence score matters and why a person still reviews low-confidence fields.
- Name the responsible-AI points: privacy of financial documents, errors, and human oversight.

## Architecture
Azure AI services account (multi-service or Document Intelligence) with a studio-based activity, a Storage account with
a `facturas` container holding **synthetic** sample invoices and receipts (no real company data). No custom app is
needed: students work in the portal's studio.

## Knobs
`sampleSet`: invoices only / invoices and receipts / adds a low-quality photo set.

## Cost and time
Pay per page analyzed, idle about $0 (or a free tier with page limits). Deploy 2 to 4 min. Lifetime: one class.

## Student activities
Analyze the samples; compare a clean PDF with a blurry phone photo; export the JSON; list which fields are wrong
and decide what a human must check.

## Student-mode policy pack
AI services account with S0 or free SKU only, one per student, public network allowed, no commitment tiers, no custom
model training.

## Build notes and risks
- **Soft delete**: deleting an AI services account keeps its name reserved; destroy must purge it.
- Sample invoices must be synthetic and generated by us; need a blob-upload content kind.
- Free tiers cap pages; with a full class, plan on the paid tier and a cost cap.
- Verify the current kind and SKU names for the account when building.
